## Step-by-Step Usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Skin" icon="droplet">
    Clean the application area with mild soap and water, then dry thoroughly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply Patch" icon="hand">
    Remove backing and press firmly onto upper arm or shoulder for 30 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Timing" icon="clock">
    Apply 30-60 minutes before bedtime for optimal effectiveness.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove" icon="trash-2">
    Gently peel off in the morning and dispose of properly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Best Practices

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Daily Routine" icon="calendar">
    - Apply at consistent times
    - Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
    - Create a relaxing bedtime routine
    - Keep room cool and dark
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Troubleshooting" icon="alert-circle">
    - If irritation occurs, discontinue use
    - Store in cool, dry place
    - Check expiration date
    - Contact support for questions
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
