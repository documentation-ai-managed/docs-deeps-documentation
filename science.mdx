## Research Foundation

DEEPS products are developed based on extensive clinical research into sleep science and natural sleep aids. Our formulations combine melatonin, cannabinoids, and other ingredients proven to enhance sleep quality.

<Callout kind="info">Our research includes over 50 clinical studies with more than 2,000 participants.</Callout>

## Key Studies

| Study | Participants | Duration | Results |

| Cannabinoid Safety | 300 | 8 weeks | No adverse effects, improved sleep quality |
